Our Philosophy

A quiet practice on Cavendish Square, where time is the first treatment we offer.

Every consultation is unhurried. Every treatment is hand-finished. And every patient, without exception, is known by name.

The Studio

Three floors of a Georgian townhouse,
quietly transformed.

Set within a restored Georgian on Cavendish Square, the studio occupies three floors of original cornicing, polished plaster walls, and low brass lighting — and behind every door, the most advanced dental technology Europe makes.

The Cavendish Experience

Four steps, at your pace.

Whether you arrive for a single hygiene visit or a year-long smile design, the rhythm is the same. Calm, considered, and quietly thorough.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    An unhurried 60 minutes. Medical history, intra-oral photographs, digital scans where useful, and time to ask anything at all. We talk first; we treat later.

  2. 02

    Bespoke plan

    A written treatment plan — options, timelines, prices, and trade-offs — laid out in plain English. You take it home. You decide. There is no pressure.

  3. 03

    Treatment

    Sessions scheduled around your calendar. Modern anaesthetic, gentle technique, optional sedation, and a clinician who explains every step before it begins.

  4. 04

    Aftercare

    Six-month reviews, hygienist visits, and a direct line to your clinician — for as long as you'd like to stay with the studio. Most patients stay for decades.
